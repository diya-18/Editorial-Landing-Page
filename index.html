I’ve always loved the magic of stepping into someone else's shoes. For me, it’s a mix of three things: the energy of live theatre, the storytelling of cinema, and the art of building a character from scratch through wardrobe and style. I’m just drawn to any medium where I can explore a new identity and make a scene feel alive
Beyond the art itself, I see performance as a powerful tool for self-expression and ownership. Stepping into a character is a way to challenge boundaries, claim space, and give a distinct, fearless voice to stories that need to be told.
